### **Headaches: Overview and Integrative Management**

#### **Epidemiology**

* **Common Types**: Migraine (with/without aura), Tension-Type Headache (TTH), and mixed headaches (\~90% of cases).  
* **Prevalence**:  
  * Migraine affects **16% of adult women** and **6% of men**.  
  * Secondary headaches (\~10%): Caused by cervical spine disorders, sinuses, TMJ issues, infections, or trauma.

#### **"Red Flag" Symptoms**

* Early morning headaches or those worsening with posture changes.  
* Explosive onset of new/severe pain.  
* Associated symptoms: mental status changes, visual dimming/double vision, syncope, fever, seizures, or focal neurological deficits.  
* Systemic illness, weight loss, HIV, or known malignancy with headaches warrant further investigation.

---

### **Migraine**

#### **Pathophysiology**

* **Characteristics**: Throbbing pain (unilateral or bilateral), nausea, photophobia, sonophobia; may last 4–72 hours.  
* **Triggers**: Menses, stress, certain foods, weather changes, and bright sunlight.  
* **Mechanisms**:  
  * **CGRP** and substance P promote vasodilation and sterile inflammation.  
  * **Serotonin** plays a role in pain prolongation.  
  * Genetic predispositions often present.

---

#### **Integrative Treatment Approaches**

**1\. Lifestyle Modifications**:

* Regular sleep, exercise, and meal patterns.  
* Stress management and ergonomic awareness.

**2\. Nutritional Interventions**:

* Eliminate common food triggers (e.g., red wine, chocolate, processed meats).  
* Increase omega-3 intake and reduce omega-6 fatty acids.

**3\. Supplements**:

* **Magnesium**: 500–1000 mg/day.  
* **Riboflavin (B2)**: 200 mg twice daily.  
* **Coenzyme Q10**: 150–300 mg/day.  
* **Botanicals**: Feverfew (125 mg daily), Butterbur (50 mg thrice daily).

**4\. Mind-Body Techniques**:

* Biofeedback, relaxation exercises, mindfulness meditation, and cognitive-behavioral therapy.

**5\. Pharmaceuticals**:

* Preventive: Amitriptyline, propranolol, topiramate, valproate.  
* Abortive: NSAIDs, triptans, ergot alkaloids, magnesium sulfate IV.

**6\. Other Therapies**:

* Acupuncture, chiropractic manipulation, massage, and aromatherapy.

---

### **Tension-Type Headache (TTH)**

#### **Pathophysiology**

* Bilateral, band-like, non-throbbing pain often linked to muscle tension in the head, neck, or shoulders.  
* Trigger points in the skull, neck, or jaw often identified.

---

#### **Integrative Management**

**1\. Lifestyle Adjustments**:

* Stress reduction, sleep hygiene, and regular aerobic exercise.  
* Adequate hydration and ergonomic corrections.

**2\. Nutrition**:

* Increase omega-3 intake; reduce sugar, caffeine, and red meat.

**3\. Supplements and Herbals**:

* Melatonin (6–10 mg at bedtime), valerian root (100–300 mg nightly).

**4\. Mind-Body Techniques**:

* Biofeedback, relaxation, mindfulness meditation, and hypnosis.

**5\. Biomechanical Approaches**:

* Manipulative therapy, craniosacral therapy, and massage.

**6\. Pharmaceuticals**:

* NSAIDs as needed, but limit use to avoid rebound headaches.

**7\. Acupuncture**:

* Regular sessions over 8–10 weeks can be effective.

---

### **Preventive Prescription Summary**

#### **Migraine:**

* Regularize sleep, meals, and exercise.  
* Avoid triggers (e.g., processed foods, alcohol).  
* Supplements: Magnesium, riboflavin, CoQ10.  
* Mind-body: Meditation, biofeedback.  
* Pharmaceuticals: Beta-blockers, anticonvulsants.

#### **TTH:**

* Address muscle tension and ergonomic issues.  
* Increase omega-3 fatty acids and hydration.  
* Utilize biofeedback, mindfulness, and physical therapy.

Both migraine and TTH benefit from a **comprehensive, integrative approach** that combines lifestyle modifications, nutritional support, mind-body techniques, and evidence-based pharmacological interventions.
