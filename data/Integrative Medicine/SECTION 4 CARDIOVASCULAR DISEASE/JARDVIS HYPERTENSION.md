### **Summary: Hypertension Management and Integrative Therapies**

---

#### **Epidemiology**

* Hypertension is a major risk factor for cardiovascular diseases, affecting over 65 million Americans.  
* Associated complications include coronary artery disease, heart failure, renal failure, aneurysms, stroke, cognitive dysfunction, and vision loss.

---

### **Clinical Guidelines and Controversies**

* **JNC 7**:  
  * Normal BP: \<120/80 mmHg.  
  * Treatment goals: \<130/80 mmHg for diabetes/renal disease.  
  * Stages: Prehypertension, Stage 1, Stage 2\.  
* **JNC 8**:  
  * Treatment thresholds:  
    * \<140/90 mmHg (18–59 years, diabetes, CKD).  
    * \<150/90 mmHg (≥60 years).  
  * Emphasis on randomized controlled trials (RCTs) for evidence, omitting observational studies.

---

### **Integrative and Non-Pharmaceutical Therapies**

#### **Lifestyle Modifications**

1. **Smoking Cessation**:  
   * Smoking increases systolic BP by \~4 mmHg.  
   * Methods: Muscle relaxation, hypnotherapy, acupuncture.  
2. **Exercise**:  
   * Aerobic exercise: Reduces BP by \~5–7 mmHg.  
   * Frequency: 30 mins/day, ≥4 days/week.  
3. **Weight Loss**:  
   * 10 lbs weight loss reduces systolic BP by \~6 mmHg.

#### **Dietary Interventions**

1. **DASH Diet**:  
   * Emphasis: Fruits, vegetables, low-fat dairy.  
   * Reduces BP by \~11.4/5.5 mmHg in hypertensives.  
2. **Mediterranean Diet**:  
   * Benefits: Lipoprotein levels, metabolic syndrome, cardiovascular health.  
   * Olive oil: Reduces systolic BP by \~5.9 mmHg.  
3. **Polyphenols**:  
   * Sources: Dark chocolate, cocoa, red wine.  
   * Cocoa reduces systolic BP by \~5 mmHg.

#### **Supplementation**

1. **Omega-3 Fatty Acids**:  
   * Dose: ≥2 g/day EPA \+ DHA.  
   * Reduction: Systolic BP by \~4.51 mmHg.  
2. **CoQ10**:  
   * Dose: 75–360 mg/day to achieve serum \>2.0 μg/mL.  
   * Reduction: Systolic BP by \~16.6 mmHg.  
3. **Magnesium**:  
   * Dose: 400–800 mg/day of non-oxide forms.  
   * Benefits: \~5.6 mmHg reduction in systolic BP.  
4. **Vitamin D**:  
   * Maintain serum level: \>40 ng/mL.  
   * Limited evidence on BP reduction.

#### **Botanicals**

1. **Garlic**:  
   * Dose: 4000 mcg allicin or 1.2 mg S-allylcysteine.  
   * Reduction: Systolic BP by \~8–16 mmHg.  
2. **Hawthorn**:  
   * Dose: 750–1500 mg/day.  
   * Mild BP-lowering effects.

#### **Mind-Body Therapies**

1. **Relaxation Techniques**:  
   * Biofeedback, transcendental meditation, mindfulness-based stress reduction.  
   * Average reduction: Systolic BP by \~10 mmHg.  
2. **Yoga, Qi Gong, Tai Chi**:  
   * Significant reductions in BP (\~10–15 mmHg).  
3. **Earthing**:  
   * Potential anti-inflammatory effects; limited evidence.

---

### **Pharmaceutical Guidelines**

* **JNC 8 First-Line Therapies**:  
  * Thiazide diuretics, calcium channel blockers (CCBs), ACE inhibitors, or ARBs.  
  * Black population: Prefer thiazides or CCBs.  
  * CKD: ACE inhibitors or ARBs.

---

### **Prevention Prescription**

1. **Dietary Goals**:  
   * DASH/Mediterranean diet, limit sodium (\<2.4 g/day).  
   * Polyphenol-rich foods: Olive oil, dark chocolate, flaxseed.  
2. **Exercise and Weight Management**:  
   * Target: ≥30 mins aerobic exercise daily.  
   * Weight loss of ≥10 lbs if overweight.  
3. **Mind-Body Practices**:  
   * Daily sessions of relaxation, yoga, or meditation.  
4. **Regular Monitoring**:  
   * Self-monitoring and follow-ups for adherence to therapy.
