### **Summary: HIV/AIDS Pathophysiology, Prevention, and Integrative Management**

#### **Pathophysiology**

* **Human Immunodeficiency Virus (HIV)** is a lentivirus causing **Acquired Immunodeficiency Syndrome (AIDS)**, characterized by progressive immune system failure.  
* **HIV-1** is more virulent and globally prevalent; **HIV-2** is less transmissible and localized in West Africa.  
* Symptoms may include **diarrhea, rash, lymphadenopathy, mucocutaneous ulcers**, or **aseptic meningitis**. Up to 60% of early-stage infections can be asymptomatic.  
* **Antiretroviral Therapy (ART)** has transformed HIV into a manageable chronic condition, significantly reducing opportunistic infections and mortality.

---

#### **Prevention Strategies**

1. **Screening**:  
   * Routine HIV testing for individuals aged 13–64.  
   * Pregnant women should be tested, including during labor if status is unknown.  
2. **Prophylaxis**:  
   * **Pre-Exposure Prophylaxis (PrEP)**: Daily Truvada reduces infection risk by up to 92%.  
   * **Post-Exposure Prophylaxis (PEP)**: ART initiated within 72 hours of exposure reduces risk.  
3. **Behavioral Interventions**:  
   * Safe sex practices.  
   * Avoiding shared needles.  
   * Smoking cessation to reduce additional health risks.  
4. **Nutrition**:  
   * A **Mediterranean diet** with omega-3 fatty acids supports immune health.  
   * Probiotics can improve absorption and maintain gut flora.

---

#### **Therapeutic Options**

##### **Pharmaceuticals**

* **ART**: Recommended for all HIV-positive individuals, regardless of CD4 count, to prevent progression and transmission.  
* **Prophylactic Agents**: Used for opportunistic infections like **Pneumocystis pneumonia** or **toxoplasmosis**.  
* **Adherence**: \>95% adherence to ART is crucial for effectiveness.

##### **Supplements**

* **Multivitamins**: Support overall health, emphasizing vitamins B, C, D, and E.  
* **L-Glutamine**: Addresses diarrhea and malabsorption.  
* **Omega-3 Fatty Acids**: Reduce triglycerides and inflammation.  
* **Zinc**: Enhances immune function; dosages must avoid excessive interference with copper absorption.

##### **Botanicals**

* **Milk Thistle**: Supports liver health, particularly in patients with hepatitis coinfection.  
* **Red Rice Yeast**: Manages hyperlipidemia linked to ART.  
* **Medical Cannabis**: Alleviates neuropathic pain but requires cautious dosing.

---

#### **Integrative and Mind-Body Approaches**

1. **Mindfulness & Stress Reduction**:  
   * Improves emotional well-being and may support immune parameters.  
   * Techniques: Meditation, progressive muscle relaxation, cognitive-behavioral therapy.  
2. **Acupuncture**:  
   * Potential benefit for immune enhancement and symptom relief; evidence is mixed.  
3. **Massage Therapy**:  
   * Enhances quality of life, reduces stress, and supports immune function.  
4. **Spirituality**:  
   * Acknowledging spiritual resources can improve coping and quality of life.

---

#### **Nutritional Recommendations**

* **Diet**: Emphasize whole foods, small frequent meals, and reduced sugar/alcohol intake.  
* **Micronutrients**:  
  * **Vitamin D**: 1000–4000 IU/day to mitigate immune deficiencies.  
  * **Calcium Carbonate**: 500 mg twice daily for ART-related diarrhea.

---

#### **Key Considerations**

* **Avoid Toxicities**:  
  * High doses of vitamin A or E may cause adverse effects.  
* **Herb-Drug Interactions**:  
  * Cytochrome P-450 inducers (e.g., St. John’s wort) can reduce ART efficacy.  
* **Monitoring**:  
  * Regular assessments of liver function, lipid profiles, and adherence.
