### **Summary: Herpes Simplex Virus (HSV) Pathophysiology, Prevention, and Management**

#### **Pathophysiology**

* **Herpesviruses**: A family of over 100 double-stranded DNA viruses, with HSV-1 and HSV-2 commonly affecting humans.  
* **Latent and Active Phases**: Symptoms appear during active periods; the virus remains dormant in sensory neurons during latency.  
* **Transmission**: Through mucosal tissue breaks, with the highest viral shedding during symptomatic phases. HSV is transmissible even during latency.

#### **Epidemiology**

* **Prevalence**:  
  * HSV-1: Affects 90% globally; increasingly found in genital regions.  
  * HSV-2: A sexually transmitted infection (STI); prevalence has decreased in the U.S. but is higher in women, African Americans, and individuals with lower socioeconomic status.  
* **Transmission Risk**:  
  * Enhanced with sexual activity, poor immunity, and triggers such as UV exposure and stress.

---

#### **Prevention and Lifestyle**

* **Avoidance**:  
  * Abstain from sexual contact during outbreaks and use condoms (30% reduction in risk).  
  * Avoid kissing during oral herpes outbreaks.  
* **Reduce Triggers**:  
  * Minimize exposure to stress, poor sleep, trauma, and UV light.  
  * Use sunblock, lip balm, and maintain good nutrition.  
* **Dietary Adjustments**:  
  * Increase lysine-rich foods (e.g., dairy, fish) and avoid arginine-rich foods (e.g., nuts, chocolate).

---

#### **Integrative Therapies**

##### **Supplements**

1. **Lysine**:  
   * Reduces outbreak frequency and severity.  
   * Dosage: 1 g orally, three times daily.  
2. **Vitamin C**:  
   * Boosts immunity and shortens symptom duration.  
   * Dosage: 500 mg twice daily during acute treatment.  
3. **Zinc**:  
   * Effective as a topical agent for reducing symptoms.  
   * Dosage: 0.25%–4% zinc sulfate cream applied during outbreaks.

##### **Botanicals**

1. **Propolis**:  
   * Effective for oral and genital herpes.  
   * Dosage: 3% ointment, four times daily for 10 days.  
2. **Lemon Balm**:  
   * Shortens healing time and reduces severity.  
   * Dosage: 1% topical ointment applied four times daily.  
3. **Aloe Vera**:  
   * Promotes faster healing of genital herpes.  
   * Dosage: 0.5% extract cream, applied three times daily for 5 days.

##### **Mind-Body Approaches**

* **Relaxation Training**:  
  * Reduces stress and HSV recurrence.  
* **Self-Hypnosis**:  
  * Demonstrated a 40% reduction in recurrences.  
* **Cognitive Behavioral Therapy**:  
  * Reduces stress and improves quality of life.

---

#### **Pharmaceuticals**

* **Primary Infection**:  
  * Start treatment within 72 hours of onset; continue for 7–10 days.  
  * Options:  
    * Acyclovir: 200 mg five times daily or 400 mg three times daily.  
    * Famciclovir: 250 mg three times daily.  
    * Valacyclovir: 1000 mg twice daily.  
* **Episodic Therapy**:  
  * Start at onset of prodromal symptoms (pain, tingling).  
  * Options for oral and genital herpes:  
    * Acyclovir, Famciclovir, Valacyclovir (doses vary by type and severity).  
* **Chronic Suppressive Therapy**:  
  * For frequent recurrences (\>6/year).  
  * Options:  
    * Acyclovir: 400 mg twice daily.  
    * Famciclovir: 250 mg twice daily.  
    * Valacyclovir: 500–1000 mg once daily.

---

#### **Therapies to Consider**

* **Black Tea Extract**: Demonstrates potential antiviral properties in vitro.  
* **Rhubarb and Sage**: Comparable healing effects to acyclovir for herpes labialis.

---

#### **Prevention Strategies**

* Honest dialogue about HSV status with partners.  
* Avoid stress, poor sleep, and trauma to skin.  
* Consider dietary modifications, lysine supplementation, and suppressive antiviral therapy.
