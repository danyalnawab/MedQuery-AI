### **Overview**

* PUD involves disruptions in the gastrointestinal (GI) mucosa, primarily due to reduced protective elements or damaging factors.  
* Common symptoms: Epigastric pain (worsens a few hours post-meal), bloating, nausea, early satiety, altered bowel habits, and heartburn. Pain improves with food or antacids.  
* Complications: GI bleeding (emergency), perforation, and, in severe cases, malignancy.

---

### **Pathophysiology**

1. **Key Contributing Factors**:  
   * **Helicobacter pylori**: Increases ulcer risk by four-fold through direct mucosal damage and triggering inflammatory responses.  
   * **Nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs)**: Inhibit prostaglandin production, reducing mucosal protection.  
   * Increased gastric acidity and inflammation.  
   * Reduced protective mechanisms: Impaired mucus production, immune function, and wound healing.  
2. **Epidemiology**:  
   * Incidence rises with age; nearly equal gender distribution.  
   * Risk factors: Family history, stress, smoking, NSAID use, and H. pylori infection.  
3. **H. pylori Discovery**:  
   * Revolutionized treatment in the 1980s; linked to ulcers, gastric cancer, dyspepsia, and more.  
   * Rates of infection vary globally: \~20% in affluent groups vs. \~60% in developing regions.

---

### **Diagnosis**

1. **Symptoms overlap with other conditions** (e.g., gastritis, GERD, Crohn’s disease).  
2. **Diagnostic Tools**:  
   * Endoscopy (gold standard) or barium GI series.  
   * Non-invasive H. pylori testing: Stool antigen, urea breath test.  
3. **Recurrent Cases**:  
   * Up to 74% of PUD cases recur; focus on prevention and management.

---

### **Integrative Treatment Approaches**

#### **Lifestyle Modifications**

* **Nutrition**:  
  * Eat **plant-based foods** rich in vitamins A and C, flavonoids (citrus, berries), and sulforaphanes (broccoli, cabbage).  
  * Incorporate probiotics (yogurt, sauerkraut) and foods like garlic, honey, and olive oil.  
  * Avoid milk (stimulates acid production) and large meals before bedtime.  
  * Avoid NSAIDs if possible.  
* **Exercise**:  
  * Routine activity reduces risk by up to 62% for duodenal ulcers.  
* **Stress Management**:  
  * Incorporate yoga, tai chi, meditation, and culturally appropriate relaxation practices.  
* **Sleep Hygiene**:  
  * Inadequate sleep increases ulcer risk due to stress-related immune dysfunction.  
* **Tobacco and Alcohol**:  
  * Avoid smoking (increases risk four-fold) and limit alcohol intake (excessive use causes mucosal damage).

---

#### **Supplements**

1. **Probiotics**:  
   * Lactobacillus or Saccharomyces boulardii reduces recurrence and aids in H. pylori eradication.  
   * Dose: At least 1 billion organisms twice daily during antibiotic therapy.  
2. **Vitamin C**:  
   * Assists H. pylori suppression and enhances antibiotic efficacy.  
   * Dose: 500 mg twice daily.  
3. **Zinc**:  
   * Promotes ulcer healing by enhancing tissue repair.  
   * Dose: 40 mg daily for 4 weeks.  
4. **Polyunsaturated Fatty Acids (PUFAs)**:  
   * Anti-H. pylori activity (e.g., fish oil, evening primrose oil).  
   * Dose: 1 g of each daily for 8 weeks.  
5. **Glutamine**:  
   * Supports mucosal healing.  
   * Dose: 400 mg four times daily.

---

#### **Botanical Treatments**

1. **Turmeric**:  
   * Anti-inflammatory and promotes ulcer healing.  
   * Dose: 600 mg five times daily for 12 weeks.  
2. **Deglycyrrhizinated Licorice (DGL)**:  
   * Effective against ulcers and H. pylori.  
   * Dose: 380 mg thrice daily for 12 weeks or 150 mg daily for 60 days for H. pylori eradication.  
3. **Mastic Gum**:  
   * Treats dyspepsia and duodenal ulcers.  
   * Dose: 350 mg thrice daily for 3 weeks.  
4. **Cabbage Juice**:  
   * High in glutamine and sulforaphanes; accelerates healing.  
   * Dose: 1 L of fresh juice daily for 10 days.  
5. **Chili (Capsaicin)**:  
   * Protective and reduces pain.  
   * Dose: 2.5 mg chili pepper capsules daily for 5 weeks.  
6. **Cranberry Juice**:  
   * Impairs H. pylori adhesion.  
   * Dose: 500 mL daily for 90 days.

---

#### **Pharmaceuticals**

1. **Antibiotics**:  
   * Typical regimen: Amoxicillin (1 g), clarithromycin (500 mg), and omeprazole (20 mg) twice daily for 14 days.  
   * Combine with probiotics to reduce side effects.  
2. **Proton Pump Inhibitors (PPIs)**:  
   * Effective for acid suppression.  
   * Dose: Omeprazole 20 mg once or twice daily for up to 8 weeks.  
3. **Antacids**:  
   * For symptom relief; follow product instructions.  
4. **Sucralfate**:  
   * Protective barrier and promotes healing.  
   * Dose: 1 g four times daily for up to 8 weeks.

---

### **Prevention Recommendations**

* **Diet**: Incorporate probiotics, flavonoid-rich foods, and vegetables like broccoli.  
* **Avoid Triggers**: NSAIDs, excessive alcohol, smoking.  
* **Lifestyle**: Manage stress, sleep well, and exercise.  
* **Early Intervention**: Address symptoms promptly to prevent complications.
