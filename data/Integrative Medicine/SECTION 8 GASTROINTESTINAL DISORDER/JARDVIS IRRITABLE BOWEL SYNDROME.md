#### **Overview**

IBS is a chronic, relapsing disorder characterized by:

* Abdominal discomfort or pain  
* Bloating and rectal urgency  
* Altered bowel habits (diarrhea, constipation, or mixed)

#### **Pathophysiology**

1. **Multifactorial Causes**:  
   * Diet, stress, altered gut-brain axis, inflammation, and microbiota imbalances.  
2. **Key Features**:  
   * Visceral hypersensitivity  
   * Altered motility  
   * Immune activation and low-grade inflammation  
   * Disrupted gut microbiota and intestinal permeability (leaky gut)

---

### **Diagnosis**

* Based on **Rome III criteria**:  
  * Symptoms present for 3+ days/month in the last 3 months.  
  * Pain linked to changes in stool frequency/form or relief with defecation.  
* Rule out other conditions: celiac disease, infections, food intolerances, inflammatory bowel disease (IBD), or colorectal cancer.

#### **Alarm Signs**

* Weight loss, fever, nocturnal bowel movements, or blood in stools require further investigation.

---

### **Integrative Management Strategies**

#### **1\. Dietary Approaches**

* **Elimination Diet**:  
  * Identify and avoid trigger foods (gluten, lactose, fructose, fatty foods).  
* **FODMaPs Diet**:  
  * Avoid fermentable carbohydrates (e.g., onions, apples, dairy).  
* **Fiber**:  
  * Soluble fiber (e.g., psyllium) improves symptoms, while insoluble fiber may worsen them.  
* **Lactose and Fructose Intolerance**:  
  * Exclude these for 2 weeks and reintroduce gradually.

---

#### **2\. Supplements**

* **Probiotics**:  
  * Bifidobacterium and Lactobacillus strains (50 billion CFU/day) reduce bloating and pain.  
* **Soluble Fiber**:  
  * Psyllium or guar gum (15 g/day) for constipation.  
* **Pancreatic Enzymes**:  
  * For suspected pancreatic insufficiency.

---

#### **3\. Botanical Therapies**

* **Peppermint Oil**:  
  * Reduces abdominal pain and bloating (1–2 enteric-coated capsules 3x/day).  
* **Ginger**:  
  * Antispasmodic effects; improves motility and reduces nausea.  
* **Combination Therapies**:  
  * Traditional Chinese Medicine (e.g., Tong Xie Yao Fang) and Tibetan formulations for IBS symptoms.

---

#### **4\. Mind-Body Interventions**

* **Stress Management**:  
  * Relaxation techniques, progressive muscle relaxation.  
* **Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT)**:  
  * Proven effective for symptom relief.  
* **Hypnotherapy**:  
  * “Gut-directed” programs reduce pain and bloating.  
* **Yoga and Exercise**:  
  * Regular physical activity and gentle yoga improve symptoms and reduce stress.

---

#### **5\. Pharmaceutical Interventions**

* **Antibiotics**:  
  * Rifaximin (400–550 mg 3x/day for 10–14 days) for SIBO.  
* **Tricyclic Antidepressants**:  
  * Low-dose amitriptyline for pain relief in IBS-D.

---

### **Prevention and Long-Term Care**

1. **Gut Health**:  
   * Avoid unnecessary antibiotics, maintain a diverse diet.  
2. **Lifestyle**:  
   * Regular exercise, quality sleep, and stress reduction.  
3. **Diet**:  
   * Reduce processed foods, emphasize plant-based eating.  
4. **Mindfulness**:  
   * Use meditation and biofeedback for long-term stress control.
