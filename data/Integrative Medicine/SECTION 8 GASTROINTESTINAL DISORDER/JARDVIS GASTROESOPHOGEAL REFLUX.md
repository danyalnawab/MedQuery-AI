#### **Overview**

* GERD occurs when stomach contents reflux into the esophagus, causing symptoms like heartburn, regurgitation, nausea, chest pain, laryngitis, and dysphagia.  
* Complications: Esophagitis, strictures, Barrett esophagus (precancerous), and adenocarcinoma.  
* Affects \~15%-20% of the U.S. population weekly.

---

### **Pathophysiology**

* **Main mechanisms preventing reflux**:  
  * Lower esophageal sphincter (LES) tone.  
  * Crural diaphragm (external sphincter action).  
  * Proper positioning of the gastroesophageal junction.  
* **GERD causes**:  
  * Dysfunctional LES tone (main cause).  
  * Increased gastric pressure (obesity, pregnancy, tight clothes).  
  * Hiatal hernia or recumbent positioning.  
  * Decreased saliva production (reduces acid neutralization).

#### **Triggers**

* Foods: Alcohol, chocolate, caffeine, citrus, tomatoes, spicy and fatty foods.  
* Lifestyle: Smoking, large meals, stress.  
* Medications: Calcium channel blockers, anticholinergics, bisphosphonates, nitrates.  
* Stress: Worsens symptoms, linked to brain–gut axis.

---

### **Diagnosis**

1. **Common tests**:  
   * Ambulatory pH monitoring (sensitivity 79%-96%).  
   * Endoscopy (to assess esophagitis, ulcers, or Barrett esophagus).  
   * Trial of proton pump inhibitors (PPIs).  
2. **Differential diagnoses**:  
   * Functional dyspepsia, esophagitis, or motility disorders.

---

### **Management Strategies**

#### **1\. Lifestyle Modifications**

* Avoid trigger foods and beverages.  
* Smaller, frequent meals; avoid eating 2-3 hours before lying down.  
* Elevate head of the bed (4-6 inches).  
* Maintain a healthy weight; quit smoking.

---

#### **2\. Botanical Therapies**

* **Demulcent herbs** (mucosa-protective):  
  * **Slippery elm**: 1-2 tablespoons root powder in water after meals.  
  * **Marshmallow root**: 5-6 g/day as tea.  
* **Anti-inflammatory herbs**:  
  * **Licorice (DGL form)**: Two 380-mg tablets before meals.  
  * **Chamomile**: 1-3 g tea, three to four times daily.  
* **Antianxiety herbs** (for stress-induced GERD):  
  * **Valerian** or **skullcap**.

---

#### **3\. Pharmaceuticals**

* **First-line**: Proton pump inhibitors (PPIs), e.g., omeprazole, taken 30 min before meals.  
* **Alternatives**: Histamine-2 receptor blockers (H2Bs), e.g., ranitidine.  
* **Adjuncts**:  
  * Over-the-counter antacids for immediate relief.  
  * Sucralfate for mucosal protection.

#### **4\. Tapering Off PPIs**

* Gradual dose reduction (2-4 weeks) to prevent rebound hyperacidity.  
* Bridge therapy with DGL licorice, slippery elm, or stress management.

---

#### **5\. Surgical Intervention**

* **Nissen fundoplication**:  
  * Wraps the stomach fundus around the esophagus to prevent reflux.  
  * Indicated for severe GERD unresponsive to lifestyle and medical therapy.

---

### **Special Considerations for Pediatrics**

* **Infants**:  
  * Symptoms peak at 4 months, often resolve by 12-24 months.  
  * Treatment: Thickened feedings, hypoallergenic formula trials, upright positioning after meals.  
* **Older children**:  
  * Avoid chocolate, mint, caffeine, citrus.  
  * Weight management and bed elevation.

---

### **Prevention Prescription**

* Avoid LES-relaxing foods and medications.  
* Stress management through mind-body practices.  
* Maintain ideal weight and adopt regular exercise routines.
