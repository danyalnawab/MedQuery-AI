#### **Introduction**

CKD affects a significant portion of the population in high-income countries (e.g., 5.3% in the UK, 13.7% in the USA). Early detection is crucial to prevent progression to ESRD.

#### **Definition**

* Persistent kidney abnormalities with GFR \<60 mL/min/1.73 m² or markers like albuminuria or hematuria.  
* eGFR is a vital tool for early detection, as serum creatinine alone may not reveal issues.

#### **Risk Factors**

* Major: Diabetes (44% of ESRD cases), hypertension (28%), obesity, smoking, and CVD.  
* Others: Prenatal conditions, environmental toxins, atopic diseases, and low magnesium.

#### **Pathophysiology**

CKD progresses via:

1. Nephron loss triggering compensatory hyperfiltration.  
2. Maladaptive changes causing oxidative stress, inflammation, and nephron damage.

#### **Conventional Management**

* Refer patients with persistent abnormalities (e.g., albuminuria, hematuria, electrolyte imbalances).  
* Manage based on KDIGO guidelines.  
* Key pharmaceutical approaches include ACE inhibitors, ARBs, diuretics, and uric acid-lowering agents like allopurinol and febuxostat.

### **Integrative Therapies for CKD**

#### **Dietary and Lifestyle Approaches**

1. **Nutrition**:  
   * Anti-inflammatory, plant-based diets rich in whole grains, fiber, magnesium, and potassium.  
   * Reduce sodium (\<2 g/day), animal protein, and processed foods.  
   * Avoid high-phosphorus additives and oxalate-rich foods.  
   * Include raw nuts (moderate amounts), probiotics, and prebiotics.  
2. **Lifestyle**:  
   * Exercise regularly (30 min/day, 5 times/week).  
   * Ensure quality sleep, manage stress, and quit smoking.

---

#### **Supplements**

1. **Magnesium**: For cardiovascular and CKD risk reduction.  
2. **Vitamin D**: Supplementation for levels \<30 ng/mL; monitor calcium/phosphate.  
3. **Omega-3 Fatty Acids**: Potential benefits for IgA nephropathy and lupus nephritis.  
4. **CoQ10**: 100–200 mg/day for potential renoprotection.  
5. **N-Acetylcysteine (NAC)**: Antioxidant effects and acute kidney injury prevention.

---

#### **Herbal and Alternative Therapies**

1. **Adaptogens**:  
   * Turmeric/Curcumin (500–2000 mg/day): Anti-inflammatory and antioxidant.  
   * Astragalus membranaceus: Renal protective properties.  
   * Cordyceps sinensis: Benefits for renal function.  
2. **Other Herbs**:  
   * Ligustrazine: Shown to improve diabetic nephropathy.  
   * Rhubarb: Used in traditional Chinese medicine for CKD.

---

#### **Mind-Body Practices**

1. **Yoga and Meditation**: Stress and oxidative stress reduction.  
2. **Tai Chi**: Renal and cardiovascular improvements.

---

#### **Environmental Precautions**

* Avoid exposure to heavy metals, pesticides, and pollutants (e.g., BPA, phthalates).  
* Limit consumption of high-mercury fish and processed foods with phosphate additives.  
* Drink pure water and use fewer plastic containers.

---

#### **Prevention and Treatment Goals**

* **Prevent CKD Progression**:  
  * Control hypertension, diabetes, and dyslipidemia.  
  * Maintain proper hydration and avoid nephrotoxins.  
* **Therapeutic Approaches**:  
  * Dietary optimization, targeted supplements, and herbal support.  
  * Lifestyle modifications with focus on stress management and sleep.

---

### **Recommendations**

1. Follow KDIGO guidelines for pharmacological interventions.  
2. Prioritize a balanced diet and lifestyle changes.  
3. Integrate safe supplements and mind-body techniques.  
4. Limit exposure to environmental toxins and processed foods.
